Robotics Engineering AI

An AI engineering assistant that helps users brainstorming, design, analysis, calculations, troubleshooting, and optimization engineering projects.

Users can describe a project, and the AI can help with:

- Design decisions
- Component selection
- Basic engineering calculations
- Troubleshooting
- Cost and performance optimization
- Safety considerations

Users can change settings such as:

- Response length
- Style
- Creativity
- Cost priority
- Performance priority
- Reliability priority
- Safety priority
- Units (metric/imperial)
- Explanation level
